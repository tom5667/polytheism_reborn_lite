# Polytheism Reborn — Changelog

> **No new game needed.** In an existing save the College of Pontiffs starts by itself: the Roman head of faith gets his College at the next 1 January at the latest. A new game shows everything from the start.

## New

- **The College of Pontiffs.** The Roman faith now has its own College, which elects the Pontifex Maximus the way the College of Cardinals elects the Pope.
  - **Who sits in it:** five pontifices, the rex sacrorum and the three major flamines (Dialis, Martialis, Quirinalis). They vote when the Pontifex Maximus dies. The ten minor flamines (Carmentalis to Palatualis) sit in the College without a vote, shown in a fold-out row of the College window. The Flamen Augustalis joins once the imperial cult has been restored.
  - **The Pontifex Maximus** holds a pontifical seat himself but does not vote. A landless Pontifex Maximus answers to no secular lord. A ruler who becomes Pontifex Maximus keeps his own realm and his own government. The Pontifex Maximus no longer replaces the core tenets of the faith.
  - **The College stays filled.** An empty pontifical seat is co-opted after about a year, and a nominated man goes first. As Pontifex Maximus you choose the rex sacrorum and the flamines yourself (character interactions "Take as Priest of the College" and "Take as Flamen Minor"). Anyone can put a Roman man forward with "Nominate for the College of Pontiffs". One seat per family, and no rivals of a sitting member.
  - **As in Rome, only men** sit in the College, and the Pontifex Maximus is always a man.
  - **Roman look:** the College window uses Roman titles and texts, the members are called Pontifex, Rex Sacrorum and Flamen, and the flamines wear the Phrygian cap. College events take place in a Roman temple.
  - Works with and without By God Alone.
- **The New Believers (Lex Ogulnia).** Converts to the Roman faith ask for a place among the pontiffs, and the Church writes to claim them back. Protect them, let them speak, or send the Church's envoy home, and then let the College vote. Opening the College adds four pontifical seats at once, or one a year. Start it with the decision "Hear the New Believers" (8 Roman counties, half of your realm Roman, and a Pontifex Maximus with at least one other pontifex).
- **Rome and Constantinople (Lex Cornelia).** The Papacy and the Ecumenical Patriarchate both court your realm, and one of your great Roman lords starts to waver. After that the College votes on fifteen pontiffs: co-opted by the College, or elected, which gives the new seats to your own great men. Start it with the decision "Bring the Great Men Before the College" (king or emperor, three quarters of your realm Roman, after the Lex Ogulnia).
  - A veto can be brought again after three years, with a better chance. After a refusal or a fourth veto the College does not hear the question again for 10 years.
- **The flamen court positions are gone** (Mars, Augustus and the eight minor flamines). The flamines are now members of the College.

## Fixes & historical accuracy

- **"Embrace … Culture" works again** for Gallo-Roman, Romano-British, Romano-Germanic and Hispano-Roman. The decisions could not be taken. They now turn you, your close family and your capital region's key county (Île-de-France, Middlesex, Cologne, Toledo) to the new culture, your vassals of your old culture get the choice, and a Cultus Deorum ruler and his family take the matching Roman rite.
- **Faith window and College window updated for CK3 1.20.1.2.** Both now include Paradox's latest changes, and the Cults tab works as before.
