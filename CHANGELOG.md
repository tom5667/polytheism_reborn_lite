# Polytheism Reborn — Changelog

## New

## Fixes & historical accuracy

- **Daco-Thracian faith reworked.** The faith now has two rites: **Zalmoxian** (the main rite, for the Dacians) and **Thracian** (formerly "Dacian", for the Thracians). The god names now follow the ancient sources:
  - Zalmoxian: Zalmoxis as the high god (also known as Gebeleizis), the Abode of Zalmoxis, Everlasting Life, the Sacred Mountain and the Teachings of Zalmoxis. Zalmoxis is no longer shown as a devil.
  - Thracian: Zbelsurdos as the high god, Bendis, the Thracian Rider, Sabazios, Kotys, Darzalas, Heros and Pleistoros. The Ursitoare and Dionysus are gone.
  - New holy sites Seuthopolis and Odessos (Varna), next to Sarmizegetusa and Philippopolis; Aidava, Capidava and Zargidava are no longer holy sites. Best seen in a new game.
  - The holy order is now called "Sons of the Rider".
- **Thracian patron gods.** "Choose Main Deity" for the Thracian rite now offers Zbelsurdos, Bendis, Sabazios, Darzalas and Pleistoros, and choosing again removes the old patron god. The offering to Zalmoxis in wartime is only for the Zalmoxian rite.
- **Old Gods in multiplayer.** The "Old Gods" decision is no longer locked for the whole world once someone starts it: every ruler follows the story on their own, so two players can each restore a different religion. After a dead end the decision returns after 10 years.
- **Activities have their own header images** again: Saturnalia, the solstice feasts (also the Germanic ones) and the pilgrimages to Kogaionon, Cnoc Uisnigh, Delphi and Vogtei.
- **Things that never worked now do:**
  - The pilgrimage to Kogaionon appears after the decision "Place Kogaionon Altar".
  - The fig offering to Juno gives its own result.
  - A restored Sol Invictus cult gets its yearly upkeep.
  - The war of a new Irish High King starts with him as the claimant.
  - The conversion menu shows either "Open" or "Close", never both, and Christians and Muslims no longer see "Open".
  - "Build Lararium" disappears once you have all seven statues.
  - A Polytheism Reborn legend (for example Hannibal, Achilles or Venus) can no longer be started a second time by someone else.
- The Roman faith now lists all cultures that have a Roman rite (Gallo-Roman, Romano-Syrian and Romano-Illyrian were missing), and the Romano cultures no longer report a missing creation date.
