# Polytheism Reborn — Changelog

> **No new game needed.** These fixes work in existing saves. Only the moved holy site of Cumae (and the Naples university it was blocking) may need a new game to show up.

## New

## Fixes & historical accuracy
- **Fewer errors in the background.** Cleared a long list of script errors found in test runs: events that created characters without a gender or a home, feasts and solstice celebrations with broken effects, wrong or outdated references to faiths, titles and traits, and several missing texts. Nothing new to see, but many events now work the way they were meant to.
- **Druid events.** "The Fading Verse" now names your court druid and gives the county a real Druidic School (it gave nothing before). The Celtic event to drive out the Sassenach can now only happen once per lifetime, as intended.
- **Roman events.** The Altar of Victory senate events create proper Christian senators again. The Temple of Vesta and the druids' gathering place now actually give their county bonus. "Restore High Priesthood" (Roman and Celtic) counts your holy sites correctly again.
- **Restore the Roman Empire.** Our version of the restoration event now always replaces the vanilla one, so the Roman options are always shown.
- **Holy site of Cumae** moved to Aversa, so Naples gets its vanilla university again.
- **Coats of arms.** The polytheist coats of arms (England, Spain, Byzantium, Athens, Rome and others) now only show for polytheist rulers. Christian, Muslim, Jewish, Zoroastrian and Eastern rulers keep the normal arms, Norman England and Hereford get their Norman arms back, and Muslim Spain its Islamic arms.
- **Feasts and pilgrimages.** Guests now see a description when invited, and a celebrated festival shows up in your faith's fervor.
- **Egyptian (Kemetic) culture** is fixed for the current game version.
- **"Invite Sassanid relative"** (only available before 790) has moved to the 700 AD bookmark mod, where these Sassanid relatives actually exist.
