# Polytheism Reborn — Changelog

A fix-up release after V4.0.0, based on what players and our own testing turned up.

## New
- **Restoring the Roman Empire** (Roads to Power): besides the Hellenic choices, you can now restore Rome with the **Roman faith** — either with the full challenge ("hard mode") or without it.
- **Faith names for newborns**: every polytheist faith now has its own list of characteristic names (kings, heroes, priests and priestesses), used by the faith button when you name a child. Caucasian, Baltic, Illyrian, Lusitanian and Scythian faiths get this button for the first time.
- **Religion quick menu**: every rite can now be picked, including the Kemetic Old Kingdom and Late Period rites, Norse, and the seven Roman regional rites.

## Fixes & historical accuracy
- **Celtic quick menu** did nothing (the tooltip said "You convert to" with no name). Fixed; every choice in the menu now sets both the faith and the rite, and the buttons show the rite names.
- **Greece** showed plain "Orthodox" instead of the Greek Rite; the Mani in the Peloponnese now correctly start in the Hellenism rite.
- **Restoring Rome**: the hard-mode challenges never actually started, and one of the game's own choices was missing. Both fixed.
- **Common names are no longer blocked for Christian characters.** Names like Zoe, Helena, Antonia or Lucia could not be given to Orthodox Greeks or Italians because a polytheist faith had claimed them.
- **Many texts showed the wrong value** because the same entry was defined twice. Cleaned up throughout — for example, the Norse patron god Freyr was shown as "Nerthus", and Dublin used its modern Irish name.
- **Old Irish dynasty names** were missing their last letter ("Conchobai", "Mái") — restored to Conchobair, Máil and so on.
- **Pre-Roman Iberians** now speak their own language, "Iberian". The game's own "Iberian Vulgar" (the later Romance language) is no longer renamed for everyone.
- **Etruscan characters** are no longer named after gods (Tinia, Laran, Turan …); a few Old Irish names that were glued together are split again; Duccidava is now a female Dacian name.
- **Emperor cultist** trait has its own description (it showed the Serapis text).
- **Puy de Dôme** holy site now sits in the right county (Clermont-sur-Allier).
- **Harpocrates** (Graeco-Egyptian patron god) bonus works again.
- **Roman faith** now allows marriage between cousins instead of between uncle and niece.

Some things may still not work as intended — please report anything odd in the bug channel on our Discord.
