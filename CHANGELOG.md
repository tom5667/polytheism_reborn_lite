# Polytheism Reborn — Changelog

**Updated for Crusader Kings III 1.21 "Florin" and Silk and Silver.**

> **Please start a new game.** CK3 1.21 rebuilt buildings, development and county control, so saves from the previous version will not carry over cleanly.

## New

- **Usury, diet and alcohol for every polytheist faith.** CK3 1.21 added these precepts. Each Polytheism Reborn religion now has them, chosen from the ancient sources:
  - **Usury:** the Roman and Greek faiths *discourage* lending at interest. Interest was legal in Rome but despised: Cato compared money-lending to murder. All other faiths *accept* it. In Mesopotamia the temples were the first lenders, and no other religion is known to have restricted it.
  - **Diet and alcohol:** no restrictions, as for vanilla pagan faiths: meat and wine stood at the heart of their sacrifices and feasts. Two rites have **Temperate Clergy** (priests abstain from meat): the **Zalmoxian** rite, after the ascetic Ctistae described by Strabo and Josephus, and the **Graeco-Egyptian** rite, after Chaeremon on the Egyptian priests of his day.
- **Polytheism Reborn buildings are districts now.** In 1.21 special buildings became districts. The Pantheons of Rome and Constantinople, the Druidic Circle, the Temple of Tanit, the first Mesopotamian brewery, the Kogaionon altar and the Irminsul are special districts, and the Oracle, the Temple of Jupiter Capitoline and the Sacred Grove are duchy capital districts. Their descriptions are filled in and corrected.

## Fixes & historical accuracy

- **Works with CK3 1.21.** Modifiers follow the new prosperity, popular opinion and county control system. Removed buildings and event themes are replaced. The faith window, the Gruesome Festival, the Iberian struggle and the Greek provinces are rebuilt on 1.21.
- **Rome is a city again.** CK3 1.21 split Rome into the city and the Vatican. The Roman decisions that need Rome (Temple of Vesta, Altar of Victory, Gates of Janus, Sibylline Books, Roman culture), the College of Pontiffs and the Pyrgi holy site now use the city of Rome.
- **Feasts** follow the new 1.21 buildings: the old farm buildings are gone, and the new palaces and halls give their discounts.
- **Restore Carthage** and **Dismantle the Papacy** follow vanilla 1.21 (Carthage now needs Tunis).
- **"Adopt State Faith"** works like vanilla again: the mod still used a copy from before 1.20.
- **"Push Out the Sassenach"** works again: it now finds the English, Saxon, German or Norse neighbour and gives the right claim.
- Cultures keep their starting innovations, and a few names and texts are corrected: Hellenic is "Hellenic" again, not "Hellenism".
