# Polytheism Reborn — Changelog

**Heads up: this is the update for Crusader Kings III 1.20.** Every religion in the mod has been rebuilt on the game's new faith-and-rite system. Saves from earlier versions are **not compatible**, so please start a new game.

## New
- **Updated for CK3 1.20.** All 15 religions now use the new faith → rite structure. Regional traditions are now rites inside a faith instead of separate faiths:
  - **Celtic**: three faiths. Ériu has the Gaelic rite, Pritani the Brythonic rite, and Continental the Gaulish and Celtiberian rites.
  - **Roman**:
    - Cultus Deorum, with regional rites for the Gallo-Romans, Romano-Britons, Romano-Germans, Romano-Hispanians, Afro-Romans, Romano-Syrians and Romano-Illyrians.
    - Etruscan is now a faith of the Roman religion.
  - **Hellenism**: Hellenic, Mycenaean and Graeco-Egyptian rites.
  - **Germanic**: Norse, Anglo-Saxon and German faiths.
  - **Baltic**: Lithuanian, Latvian and Prussian rites.
  - **Mesopotamian**: Babylonian, Assyrian and Sumerian rites.
  - **Caucasian**: Armenian and Georgian rites.
  - **Egyptian**: Old Kingdom, Kemetic and Late Period rites, plus Atenism as its own faith.
  - **West Semitic**: Canaanite and Punic rites.
  - **Daco-Thracian**: Dacian and Zalmoxian rites.
- **Researched god names and holy sites for every religion.** They are checked against ancient sources, such as inscriptions, ancient authors, Linear B for the Mycenaeans and Herodotus for the Scythians. Each rite shows its own gods, whoever is looking at it.
- **Two new cultures: Romano-Syrian and Romano-Illyrian.** Both have attested names and are available in the quick culture menu.
- **Virtues and sins** of every religion now use the 1.20 weighting system and are historically sourced.
- **Patron gods:**
  - Every rite opens its own "Choose Main Deity" menu, with gods that match that rite, and the Mesopotamian faiths get a menu of their own.
  - Patron gods are now character bonuses with their own icons.
- **Festivals and pilgrimages:**
  - Saturnalia and the Celtic solstice feasts end with their own festival event.
  - The pilgrimage to the Hill of Uisneach has its own arrival story.
- **New events now in the game:**
  - the druid as judge and as teacher;
  - an offering to the Lares at the household shrine;
  - an offering to Demeter;
  - letters for the Vestal Virgins invitation.

## Fixes & historical accuracy
- **Vanilla features work again.** The mod used to replace several game files with outdated copies, and these are now removed or rebuilt on 1.20:
  - council positions;
  - rite growth, heresy and faith creation;
  - head-of-faith events;
  - the Gruesome Festival and the Iberian struggle;
  - the decisions to restore Rome, restore Carthage and dismantle the Papacy.
- **Doctrines load again.** The patron-god doctrines and the "Interpretatio" doctrines now work. The Interpretatio doctrines make the Roman, Greek, Celtic, Germanic and other faiths friendlier to each other.
- **Old faiths fixed.** Dozens of decisions, events and conversion interactions pointed at faiths that no longer existed; they work again.
- **Events and texts.** Events that never fired are now connected or removed, and missing event texts were written.
- **Names with accents or macrons now display properly**, for example Aurēlius, Dumnorīx and Ābar, instead of showing as raw text.
- **Bonuses that silently did nothing now work.** This covers the Flamen court positions and many county control, piety and prestige bonuses.
- **Romano-X cultures** now get their own regional rite instead of plain Cultus Deorum.
- **Pergamon and Galatia** now belong de jure to the Byzantine Empire.
- **Removed** the "Demand to convert to Greco-Roman" interactions, which never worked. Use the Cultus Deorum or Hellenism conversion instead.
- **Many smaller fixes** to scripts and texts that broke when loading.
